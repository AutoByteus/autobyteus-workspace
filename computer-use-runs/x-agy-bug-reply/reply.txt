Bug report for Antigravity CLI (agy, headless, --output-format stream-json):

After a turn hits a real quota error (429 RESOURCE_EXHAUSTED, "Individual quota reached… Resets in 46m49s"), that conversation never recovers. Every later turn ends with status ERROR and the exact same message, still "Resets in 46m49s", days later.

The turns actually succeed: the agent does all its work and returns a full response, and the agy logs show no new 429s. It even happens on trivial no-tool turns, and it persists across agy restarts.

It looks like the stored error step from the original 429 in the conversation history is being reported as the result of every later turn.

Seen on agy 1.2.16. The only workaround so far is starting a new conversation.
