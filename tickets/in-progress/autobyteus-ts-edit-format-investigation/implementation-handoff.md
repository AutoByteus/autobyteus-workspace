## Important Assumptions

- A header delimiter is recognized only after removing its supported line ending, never after trimming its content prefix. It must be an unprefixed bare `@@` or exactly `@@ -N[,N] +N[,N] @@`; whitespace-padded headers, textual labels, partial headers, and alternate spacing remain unsupported.
- A hunk's unchanged/removal sequence is line-based and must identify exactly one location at or after the cursor consumed by prior hunks.
- Existing missing-definition resolution remains the authority for persisted names; no external agent definition is rewritten.
- Delivery, not implementation, owns the final refresh against `origin/personal`.
