## Conversations stay in order after a restart

- After the application restarts, a restored agent's conversation no longer merges its replies before and after the restart: every message and reply shows once, live and after you reload, on the desktop and the phone.
- The restart reminder, and every other platform notice, now shows once per notice instead of twice.
- The restart reminder card shows the restart time in your own time zone, for example "18 Nov 2026, 10:35 CET". The agent still receives the time in UTC.

### Good to know

- Agents restored before this update keep the turns they already stored. In those older conversations, replies from before this update can still appear merged, and their older restart reminders behave as before. Everything they do from this update on shows correctly. Starting a new conversation also avoids it.
