## What's New
- **Settings on the phone.** A run's ⋮ menu has **Settings**: change a stopped run's runtime and model, as on the desktop; the change applies with your next message. A run that is still live shows the red stop button there: one tap stops it (a Team or Org member stops its Team or Org), and you can then change it.
- **Text size on the phone.** The ⋮ menu has Small, Standard, Large and Largest; your choice applies at once and is kept on the phone.

## Improvements
- The phone's conversation is easier to read: wider text, a larger type size, and each speaker's name and picture shown once per turn. Messages from other agents look as on the desktop.
- The message box has rounded lower corners on the desktop and the phone.

## Fixes
- Stopping a run no longer reports a failure when the node did stop it, on the desktop and the phone.
