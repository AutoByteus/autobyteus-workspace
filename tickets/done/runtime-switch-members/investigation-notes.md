| F-01 | **Why the first delivery was a single agent.** A standalone run keeps its provider binding in its own `run_metadata.json` (`runtimeKind`, model, `platformAgentRunId`), edited by `StandaloneStoppedRunSelectionUpdater` and activated by `StandaloneAgentRunLifecycleService`. The team recommended the single run as the smaller slice; the user accepted it (2026-10-30). | Read: `agent-run-metadata-types.ts`, `run-execution-tree-shared-records.ts`, `docs/modules/agent_team_execution.md` |
| F-02 | **One activation class, two construction sites (corrected by ARCH-REV-001 F-002).** `ConfiguredAgentExecutionHandle` owns the provider/local mechanics of every configured or task AgentRun (`initializeReady`: plan and prepare a candidate, `callbacks.commitPlatformBindingChange(change)`, `commitPublication`; `handle:354-390`). | F-05 | **Delegated copies.** The user's choice per copy is `userLaunchSelection {llmModelIdentifier, llmConfig, selectedAt}` on the agent-level record and `userTeamLaunchSelection` on a Team-level record, beside the started launch which is never changed (DEC-005, AR-005). `effectiveCopyAgentLaunch` is the one rule (own choice, else the Team's for a follower, else started); `withCopyLaunch` applies it to a `TeamRunAgentNode` (model and settings only today).
