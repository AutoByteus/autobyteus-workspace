## What you need to do

- **iPhone and iPad: update from TestFlight.** Open TestFlight and update AutoByteus to the new build (version 1.5.14, build 343). It adds **Try demo** on the first screen, so you can look at the whole app with sample agents, teams and files before you connect a computer. This is a TestFlight beta; nothing is submitted to the App Store or to Apple's app review.
- **If you saved a computer in an earlier version of the iPhone app,** it keeps working. A computer that was saved without a name shows as "AutoByteus node" in the app's own list until you scan its QR code again.
- **Nothing else to do.** The desktop app, the Android app and your server nodes need no action for this release, and nothing is converted on first start.

**Full Changelog**: https://github.com/AutoByteus/autobyteus-workspace/compare/v1.5.14-beta.2...v1.5.14-beta.3
