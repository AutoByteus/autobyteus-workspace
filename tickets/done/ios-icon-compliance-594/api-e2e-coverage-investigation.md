## Current Requirement And Design Basis

- **REQ-001/002, AC-001: the icon set.** All 18 slots are generated by `autobyteus-web/scripts/build-ios-app-icon.mjs` from `public/autobyteus-icon.svg`: the mark on a full square of the circle's fill (#1E3A8A), at each slot's exact pixel size, as 8-bit RGB without alpha.
- **REQ-003, AC-002/003: the drift check.** `ios-icon:check` compares every slot to a fresh render (max channel 8, mean 0.5) and names the failing slot. The release workflow's `build-ios` job runs it after the install, before the build.
- **REQ-004, AC-004: export compliance.** `Info.plist` declares `ITSAppUsesNonExemptEncryption` = false. The contract check reads the PNGs' chunks, the key and the workflow step.
- **REQ-005, AC-005:** delivery deletes the 592 branch.

Out of scope: the desktop and Android icons, the store artwork, a release, tinted icons, and the `Contents.json` slots themselves (FU-001). App Store Connect's own compliance screen is observable only at the founder's next upload; it is not an AC.

- Designer scenarios covered:
  - SCN-001: the home-screen icon on iPhone and iPad.
