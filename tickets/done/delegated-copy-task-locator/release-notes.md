# Release Notes — delegated-copy-task-locator

## New

- **See each delegated copy's Task in the Workspaces tree.** Under Agent runs and Agent Team runs, a delegated Agent
  or Team copy now shows a one-line summary of the Task it is working on, right under its name.
- **Jump from a copy to its Task.** Click the summary, or press Enter or Space on it, to open the Projects tab in the
  right panel on that Task. The right Project or Temp tasks is chosen, the card is scrolled into view, focused and
  briefly highlighted, and your conversation stays where it is.
  - It works with the panel docked or hidden, and in the narrow-window drawer.
  - It also works from pages without a right panel, such as Agents: the run opens first.
- **Summaries stay current.** A summary updates without a reload when its Task is renamed, closed, deleted, or
  replaced by a follow-up Task.

## Fixed

- The Cancelled status pill now shows its intended light slate ring.
- A two-line tree row now leaves the tree smoothly instead of jumping before it collapses.
