2. `DEC-002`: With current Vertex Express mode, whose published REST resources omit Voices API, should creation/management show an explicit unavailable state until a documented capable AI Studio/full Vertex Project mode is selected (recommended), or should there be a separately selected voice-service credential? 3. `DEC-003`: Confirm whether **both** voice design and adult-consent replication are intended in this first round, and whether stateless voice keys, custom-voice dialogue assembly, streaming and output encoding/rate controls may remain separate follow-up scope.

Only after those decisions and explicit approval of the complete requirements/ACs may Solution Designer produce a design and classify size/risk. No implementation-ready handoff exists now.
