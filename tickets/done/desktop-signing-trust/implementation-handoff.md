### IR-002 delta: genuine first use, not merely empty settings

- The original reviewer diagnostic reproduced on 72d59d7796 before changes. Missing configuration alone used to reach the claim even if release inspection was inaccessible.
- The new desktop-first-use reader inspects complete, counted pages of official release asset names (including legacy and partial sidecars), plus official Desktop Release attempts from the introduction day of the managed signer (all events, not just successful pushes). Known unsigned workflow bytes are distinguished from the reviewed managed workflow. Every managed attempt must be complete with a skipped signer. Previously observed bootstrap release/run must still be accounted for. Signed records, executed signer, unknown code, inaccessible/changed/oversize history and other ambiguity refuse before claim/key/backup/upload. An ordinary unsigned legacy AppImage or unrelated asset is not a conflict. This eligibility check does not require a positive signing witness.
- The generator rechecks settings and local destination after history inspection and before the claim. Rotation still requires the original positive witness; the published positive proof is **not** used to authorize fresh provisioning. The release guide now explains reconciliation. No production generator, rotation, backup handling or release actions in IR-002.
- Inspection limits: up to 1,000 releases, 100 assets per release, 40 post-introduction runs, 64 managed attempts. Missing known history or hitting an uninspectable boundary requires manual reconciliation; absence cannot defeat an uncooperative administrator or guarantee historical records were never erased.
