# Release Notes — Compact Task cards

## Fixed

- **Task cards no longer show a Task's entire description.** On Project boards and on Temp tasks, a card now shows at most two lines of its summary and two lines of its context, ending with "…".
  - Long agent-written briefs no longer fill the screen, and boards with many Tasks stay scannable.
  - The worker line, context-file count and live highlights are unchanged.
  - Open a Task to read its full description. Search still finds words anywhere in the description.
- The Task delete confirmation and screen-reader labels use a short version of the summary instead of the whole first line.

## Upgrade notes

- Presentation only. No data or behavior changes.
