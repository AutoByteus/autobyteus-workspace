## Platform notices get their own row

- Notices from the application, such as "Application restarted", "Background tasks finished", "Skill updated", "Tools updated" and "Agent runtime changed", now appear as their own light row in the conversation, with an icon and a label. They no longer sit inside the agent's message.
- On the phone, the agent's name and picture appear again after a notice, so you can see who is speaking.
- The label replaces the bracketed "[Platform notice — …]" line.
