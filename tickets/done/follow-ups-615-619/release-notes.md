## The Memory page lists a Team inside an Org under its Org

- On the Memory page, the Agent Teams list and each team's runs showed a Team that belongs to an Org as if it were an Agent Team of its own, since Orgs began to hold their Teams as runs of their own. The page now lists only real team runs in the Agent Teams view. A Team inside an Org appears under its Org, with its members' memory, as before.
- Nothing is changed for standalone Teams or for the Org's own page.
