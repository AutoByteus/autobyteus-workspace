# Contributing to AutoByteus

Thank you for your interest in AutoByteus. Contributions are welcome.

## How to contribute

- For larger changes, open an issue first so the approach can be agreed before
  you invest time in it.
- Open pull requests against the `personal` branch.
- Follow the repository instructions in [`AGENTS.md`](./AGENTS.md), the design
  principles in [`DESIGN.md`](./DESIGN.md) and the testing guideline in
  [`TESTING.md`](./TESTING.md).

## Licensing of contributions

AutoByteus is dual-licensed; see [`LICENSING.md`](./LICENSING.md). Contributions
to the AGPL components are accepted under the Contributor License Agreement
(CLA), which lets the copyright holder offer them under AGPL-3.0-only and under
commercial licenses. Contributions to the permissively licensed SDK and contract
packages are also accepted under the CLA.

## Contributor License Agreement required

Outside contributors must accept the [AutoByteus CLA](./CLA.md) before their
pull request can be merged. To accept, tick the CLA checkbox in the pull request
description, or comment the acceptance sentence given in the CLA. Pull requests
are merged only after the CLA has been accepted.

The copyright holder's own contributions, and those made by his AutoByteus
agents, are exempt.
